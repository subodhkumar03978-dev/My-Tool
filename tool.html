About Photo & Signature Cropper & Resizer

Photo & Signature Cropper & Resizer is a browser-based utility for cropping, resizing and compressing photos and signatures for online form requirements. Image processing is performed locally in your browser. Files selected for processing are not uploaded to our server by this tool.

This website is an independent utility and is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by SSC, Railway, IBPS, or any government department.
